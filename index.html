1. Privacy Promise

🔒
FinLedger does not sell personal data. We are designed to process financial information locally on your device and avoid unnecessary collection.

This Privacy Policy explains how FinLedger collects, uses, stores, and protects personal and financial information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. SMS Data and Financial Message Processing

To support automated transaction categorization, budget tracking, bill monitoring, and personal finance insights, FinLedger may request access to SMS messages related to financial activity.

  • Local-only processing: Financial SMS data is analyzed on your device using local parsing logic and stored in secure local app storage.
  • No remote upload by default: We do not transmit raw SMS content, transaction details, account numbers, or merchant data to external cloud servers as part of normal app operation.
  • Non-financial messages: OTPs, promotional messages, personal communications, and unrelated non-financial SMS are filtered and ignored.
  • Purpose-limited use: SMS parsing is used only to identify and summarize financial activity for budgeting and analytics in the app.

3. Information We Collect

Directly provided information

Information you provide voluntarily, such as app settings, preferences, feedback, support requests, and optional account-related details.

Automatically collected information

Device model, Android version, locale, timezone, application version, and diagnostics needed to improve the product.

  • Financial records and category data created within the app.
  • SMS-based transaction information when SMS access is granted.
  • Issue reports, crash logs, and technical diagnostics when you voluntarily submit them.

4. Third-Party Services

FinLedger may use trusted third-party services to support app reliability, analytics, and advertising.

  • Google AdMob: Advertising services may process device identifiers and ad-related metadata to serve relevant ads as permitted by applicable platform policies.
  • Firebase services: We may use Firebase for diagnostics, crash reporting, and remote configuration when necessary for product stability and feature deployment.
  • No user financial database: We do not maintain a centralized personal finance database for your transactions unless you explicitly choose to upload or share data.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • Financial data is stored locally on your device in secure app storage.
  • We apply reasonable security practices to protect data transmitted to approved third-party services.
  • We retain information only as long as needed to provide services, maintain security, and comply with applicable law.
  • You may remove app data through app settings or by clearing app storage from your device settings.

6. User Rights and Control

You have control over your privacy choices in the app and on your device.

  • Manage SMS access permissions from Android settings.
  • Delete local app data or uninstall the app at any time.
  • Reset or disable advertising identifiers in your device settings.
  • Request clarification about how the app handles your data through the appropriate app or platform channels.

7. Children’s Privacy

FinLedger is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18 without legal parental consent where required.
